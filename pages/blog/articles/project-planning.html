Start with a clear project brief

Include the stand number, suburb, and municipality (e.g., City of Harare or Bulawayo City Council).

Project sponsor & team

List the Client, Architect, Quantity Surveyor (QS), Structural Engineer, Main Contractor, and Site Agent.

Define scope and constraints

Clearly separate core structural deliverables from optional finishes to prevent scope creep.

Contract framework

Specify the contract type (such as ZGCC or JBCC), Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and standard 10% retention terms.

Zimbabwe regulatory & pre-construction checklist

Before breaking ground, your plan must account for local urban planning laws and statutory approvals. This includes the following steps:

  • Secure an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) from the Environmental Management Agency (EMA), if required.
  • Register the site with the National Social Security Authority (NSSA) factories inspectorate.
  • Apply for temporary water and power connections through the local council and ZETDC.
