Privacy Policy

How the website handles information.

Last updated: 11/11/2026

Introduction

This website is operated by EMROD CREST ("we", "us", or "our"), a building construction, extension, and finishing service provider based in Zimbabwe. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling any personal information you share with us responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07].

Information collected

Because this is a static informational website, we do not automatically collect personal profiles when you browse. Information is only collected or processed in the following ways:

  • Direct Inquiries: When you contact us via phone, email, WhatsApp, or a quote request form, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, project location (suburb or stand details), and project scope.
  • Technical Hosting Logs: Our web hosting provider may automatically record standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps) strictly to maintain website security, performance, and reliability.
  • Browser Storage: Your browser stores your light or dark theme preference locally via localStorage. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Use and sharing

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiries, prepare project estimates or site assessments, deliver contracted construction services, and communicate project updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third parties for marketing.

Information may only be shared with trusted project partners (such as architects, engineers, quantity surveyors, or material suppliers) when necessary to prepare your quote or execute your project, or where required by law.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry and project records only for as long as necessary to fulfill your request, manage ongoing construction works, or meet statutory accounting and contractual obligations. We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to protect client communications and project documents against unauthorized access.

Your choices and rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for inaccurate details to be corrected, or request deletion of your inquiry records where we are not legally or contractually required to retain them. You may also opt out of follow-up communications at any time.
