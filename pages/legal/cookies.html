Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: 11/11/2026

Cookie use

This is a static website and does not set first-party tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. To improve your browsing experience, the site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to remember your display theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode) as you move between pages. This setting remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-party services

Our website contains outbound links to external platforms such as Google Maps (for viewing our pinned location), WhatsApp, and official regulatory portals. When you click these links and leave our website, those third-party services operate under their own privacy and cookie policies.

Managing preferences

Because we do not use non-essential or tracking cookies, no cookie consent banner is required. You can clear or block browser storage and cookies at any time through your web browser's privacy or site-data settings. Clearing site data will reset your display theme to the default dark mode.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or how this website handles browser storage, please reach out via our Contact page.
